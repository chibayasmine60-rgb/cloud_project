Faculties and departments

Our Departments

Explore the university's seven departments and their teaching focus.

Biology

The Biology Department focuses on the study of living organisms and their environment. Students explore cell biology, genetics, microbiology and ecology.

Chemistry

The Chemistry Department studies the composition, properties and reactions of different substances. Students learn organic and inorganic chemistry through lessons and laboratory work.

Computer Science

The Computer Science Department focuses on programming, software development, databases and computer networks, helping students build modern technical skills.

Languages

The Languages Department helps students improve their English and French through grammar, literature, writing, speaking and translation.

Mathematics

The Mathematics Department covers algebra, calculus, geometry and statistics while developing logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Medicine

The Medicine Department focuses on the human body, anatomy, diseases and medical sciences, preparing students for careers in healthcare.

Physics

The Physics Department explores the laws of nature through mechanics, electricity, magnetism, optics and practical experiments.
